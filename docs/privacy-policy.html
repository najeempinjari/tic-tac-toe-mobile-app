Privacy Policy — Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Najeem Pinjari ("we", "our", or "us") handles information in the Tic Tac Toe mobile application ("the App"). The short version: we do not collect, store, or sell your personal data.

Information We Collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, location, camera, or microphone data. Game state and preferences remain locally on your device.

Over-the-Air Updates & Technical Data

To deliver bug fixes and app updates, the App uses Expo Updates (provided by Expo / 650 Industries, Inc.). When the App checks for updates, standard network request data—such as your device's IP address, operating system version, and app version—may be temporarily processed by Expo's servers solely to deliver the update. This information is not used to identify you personally.

Open-Source Software & Third-Party Services

The App is built using standard open-source libraries (such as React Native and Expo SDK packages) that operate locally on your device without transmitting personal data. The App contains no third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties, except for the standard technical network requests required to check for app updates as described above.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future — for example, if new features require data collection — this page will be updated, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
